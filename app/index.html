DompetRumah — a household budget planner. The Prameswari household is a worked example: 2 adults, 2 children, renting in East Jakarta on Rp9.929.876 a month. Figures are the household's own; national benchmarks come from BPS. Everything on this site runs from its own files — no network, no account, nothing to sign up for.
